A routine with room for real life.

We plan for one Sunday and two other cleaning days each calendar month. Your recorded jobs guide what comes next. Miss a week? The schedule slips: unfinished jobs carry forward while sessions stay at the usual workload.

Starting fresh? Kitchen mopping starts in the first month and repeats monthly. Other rooms start mopping over three months, with vacuuming starting within two. You can record earlier cleaning in “What I did”.
